## Wordplay (`redesign/HomeWordplay.kt`, strings `values/strings_home_redesign.xml`, tests `HomeWordplayTest`)

- Pure picker, no Android. Time buckets: morning 5-11, afternoon 12-16, evening 17-21, late night 22-4. A song with
  >= 5 plays this week (`STREAK_MIN_PLAYS`, the week's real top song from the stats overview) wins: the STREAK set.
- `rotate(day, size, salt) = (day * step + offset) mod size`, step coprime with size: deterministic per local day and
  set, never the same line two days running, every line comes round. Salts separate greeting sets / sections / sub line.
- Greeting = two string arrays per set (`home_wp_<set>_a` wide line, `_b` narrow line), 3 lines each. Sections:
  `home_wp_mixes_*`, `home_wp_recent_*`, `home_wp_fresh_*` (accent part keeps its leading space).
- Sub line only with a real count: plurals `home_wp_plays_0/1/2` with this week's total plays. Time chip: the time
  (system 12/24 h format), plus "· <song> on repeat" in the streak set. Nothing invented; no count = no line.
