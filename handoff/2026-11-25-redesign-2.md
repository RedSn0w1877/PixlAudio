## Font

- `FlexAxis.ROND_HOUSE = 0f`: Google Sans Flex with flat terminals everywhere (GoogleSansRounded,
  FlexSpec default, chip preset, top bar title, mix/recents titles, genre cards, artist rank
  numerals). Lyrics already used ROND 0.
- The fit-to-width policy now uses the font's whole wdth range read from `gflex_variable.ttf`'s
  fvar (25..151, default 100): `FlexFitPolicy()` = min 25, max 151, wrap cap 100, step 5 (27
  instances, warmed off the main thread). The collapsed hero squishes to wdth 70 (was 84).
- iOS (SF Pro) has no ROND; its width range is its own (`.compressed`..`.expanded`).
