# Equalizer and lyric timing, November 19, 2026

On `fix-eq-timing`, open Equalizer from Settings or the sidebar. The shared Android and iOS layout puts the current preset and power switch above the frequency editor. Sliders, Graph and Hybrid are directly selectable. You can edit the curve with the filter bypassed. The screen groups the effect switches and strength controls below the editor. Output volume still follows Spotify Connect when connected.

MusicService restores the saved switches at startup. Named custom presets restore the same curve in the audio engine and the editor, including old presets with fewer bands. Device effect availability is checked for each playback session so an unavailable effect does not disable its neighbors.

For a song with synced lyrics, More → Adjust sync opens the timing strip. Tap the 100 ms or 500 ms buttons to move the lyric clock immediately in either direction. The middle button resets the offset. More → Hide sync controls closes the strip.

The JVM regressions are in `LyricsClockTest` and `EqualizerPresetTest`. The pull request tracks the Gradle build, JVM tests and physical phone checks. On the phone, exercise both Equalizer entry points, edit with the filter bypassed, switch it on to audition the curve, and reopen a saved preset after restarting. In lyrics, check both nudge sizes and reset while playing and paused.
