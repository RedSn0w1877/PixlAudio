## What the owner reported, and what was done

> "the first like haptic tap was a good point to send data at ... it takes a while to fully register"

Why it was slow (spec 4): the haptic fired at the detector's fire, and only then did the receiver read the signed offer over GATT,
check it, ask (first beam), save (a library write, or a network search for a song with no video), write Ack, and the sender
answer `go` with the beams starting 150 ms after that. Everything slow came after the tick.

Now the slow part is done before the tap (**prefetch**) and the tap is the commit:

1. The sender's listener says yes to a phone ("Song drop to X?"). Only then is an offer published, for that phone only.
2. The receiver is already connected (it connects when the sender is inside the strict near gate). It has written `Hello`, read the
   sender's `Name`, and reads `Offer` the moment the sender says it has one (`EVENT_OFFER_READY` notification, with a 2 s retry as a
   safety net). It verifies frame, signature, challenge and freshness and **holds the result** (`OfferPrefetched` event; core state `pre`).
3. At the fire the core does, in ONE reduction, in this order: `haptic(tick)`, `saveSong`, `scheduleTimeout(save)`, `startBeam(receive, now)`.
   No read, no ack, no `go`, no delay. If the first-beam question is due the order is `haptic(tick)`, `scheduleTimeout(consent)` and the beam
   starts at Add.
4. The sender commits at its own fire (or when the receiver's `Tap` message arrives and its own signal jumped in the last 1.5 s) with
   `haptic(tick)`, `startBeam(send, now)`, `scheduleTimeout(ack)`, **provided the target already read the offer**; if the tap comes first the
   beam starts when the read arrives.
5. The receiver's `Ack` (after its save) and `Decline` (not found, failed, limits) are the sender's result: the sender's session ends when
   its beam has played AND the Ack is in (45 s timeout otherwise).

**Effect on the two timelines (replaces the handshake).** There is no `go` and `GO_DELAY_MS` is 0. Each phone starts its own beam at the moment it
commits. The two animations therefore differ by the difference of the two commit times: each phone commits on its own signal event, or the sender on
the receiver's `Tap` message (one GATT write, tens of ms). Expected skew: tens of ms, rarely over 150 ms. The motion is symmetric, eased and the
phones are touching, so it should not show; nothing corrects it (unverified on devices). The `SongTapHandshake` object keeps only
`impulseAgeMs`, `peerImpulseLocalMs`, `MAX_RTT_MS` and `GO_DELAY_MS = 0`.

**Gentle holds.** The user brings the phones together and holds; they do not bump. The detector has a third fire path, `FireReason.HOLD`: the
smoothed signal at or above `CLOSE_HOLD_DBM` (-52) for `CLOSE_HOLD_MS` (350 ms), only inside the strict near gate, with or without a jump or a jerk.
The jerk (accelerometer) is now an optional second factor: it only makes the jump path fire sooner (150 ms instead of 400/350). After any fire the hold
timer is consumed (`DetectorState.closeSinceMs = CLOSE_USED = -2`) until the phones come apart (the signal leaves the near gate), so one contact is
one fire. All safety rules stay: consent for the first beam and every fifth auto-saved beam, the rate limits, the mute after two undos, the strict payload
rules, the signed offer bound to the receiver's challenge, and the new rule that nothing is offered without the sender's yes.

> "it can find phones too easily like im far enough away and its still connected"

Causes: advertising at MEDIUM power, a loose absolute gate (-70 enter) that decided connecting, serving and firing, and the picker did not exist.

- **Advertising power**: `BleTuning.ADVERT_TX_POWER = 1` = `ADVERTISE_TX_POWER_LOW` (-15 dBm), was MEDIUM. ULTRA_LOW (0) is the next step down; the strict gate was
  chosen for LOW, so if it is lowered the gate needs calibrating again.
- **Strict near gate** (pure core, `SongTapTuning`): `NEAR_ENTER_DBM = -55`, `NEAR_EXIT_DBM = -63` (were -70 / -78). It gates connecting (receiver), serving the offer
  (sender), and every fire. It is a coarse pre-gate only; the tap itself is still the relative detector (jump, jerk, hold), never a fixed dBm.
- **List gate** (separate, broader): `LIST_ENTER_DBM = -78`, `LIST_EXIT_DBM = -85`. Used only by the picker list and built from advertisements only
  (no connection). A phone at a distance can be listed; connecting and beaming still need the strict gate.
- **Sender serves the offer** only when: it was armed to that phone, that phone is the one being followed, it is inside the strict gate, no other receiver is
  inside the strict gate (a connection's own address is not the advertising address, so "which phone is this client" cannot be known; with two close, nobody is served),
  the client sent a Hello and no other client read it.
- Scan: still `SCAN_MODE_LOW_LATENCY` with the two service UUID filters (the jump needs the readings); over-reporting is handled by the gates in software.
- **The diagnostics screen shows the gates** and which one is active ("gate now": strict near / list only / outside; "may connect and beam"; "shown in the picker"; "offer in hand";
  advertised and full name), the three thresholds and the advert power, and "Copy log" has them.
- **Calibration source**: there are no logs from the owner's Mark buttons anywhere in the repo or the handoffs, so the numbers are conservative guesses,
  **to be calibrated on the owner's phones**: strict -55/-63, list -78/-85, hold -52. My estimate (not measured): at contact with LOW advertising an Android phone reads about -45;
  an iPhone advertises at a higher fixed power, so the same gate admits it from further away; the two directions will differ.

> "add a naming system ... song drop to X? with a yes or no ... a song drop option [next to Share] ... tap phones together to share"

- **Your name** (Settings > Song Tap): `SongTapSettings.name` (DataStore `song_tap`, key `your_name`), default `Build.MODEL` ("Pixel 10 Pro"), 1 to 20 Unicode scalars, trimmed,
  spaces collapsed, control / zero width / bidirectional / tag characters removed (`SongTapNames.clean`, built on the payload validators in `SongTapText`). Empty
  after cleaning puts the device name back. Dialog: `SettingsSongTap.NameDialog`.
- **Cards say who**: the receiver's consent card and the beam card show "From <name>" (`SongTapUiState.peerName`, the full name from the Name characteristic, the short advertised one until it is read).
  The sender's armed card says "Sharing with <name>".
- **Prompt on the sender**: `SongTapUiState.prompt` (a phone inside the strict gate that was not told no, `NearbyBook.promptFor`) opens "Song drop to X?" Yes / No on the Song Tap screen
  (Send). Yes = `SongTapStateHolder.approve` (arms the beam to that phone only). No = `decideNo` = that phone is not asked about for 60 s.
- **Picker**: the Song Tap screen (Send) lists "Phones nearby" (`NearbyBook.visible`: strongest first, ties by number, stale after 4 s, one row per phone, a row with the same name
  whose newer twin appeared more than 1.5 s later is dropped as an address change). Tapping a row asks the same question. Yes shows "Tap phones together to share" with the target's name
  and the beam artwork; Cancel (or 60 s, `TARGET_TIMEOUT_MS`) lets go.
- **Song Drop button** in the song options sheet's big button row, a round tertiary button right of Share (`SongInfoBottomSheet.Row1Actions`, `songdrop_button_cd`). It calls
  `SongDropViewModel.start(song)`; `AppNavigation` collects `dropRequests`, collapses the player sheet and opens `Screen.SongTap` as a sender. The picked song (not the one playing) is what is offered
  (`SongTapStateHolder.dropSong`) until the screen is left. It is in every place the sheet appears (library, search, albums, ... and the player). If Song Tap is off, the screen shows its existing
  "Turn on Song Tap" card (it also asks for Bluetooth).
- **The receiver must have Song Tap open on Receive**: the radio is armed only while that screen is resumed (unchanged privacy rule), so a phone that is not on that screen is not in the list.
  The picker's empty text says so.
- The old "touch and it sends" without a yes is gone for Bluetooth: a sender never beams to a phone its listener did not say yes to. The QR code route is unchanged.

## For the iOS port

Everything here is exact. The goldens cover all of it (names, nearby, wire, session, detector).

### Core changes (port into `Packages/PixlCore/Sources/PixlLibrary/SongTap/`)

`SongTapTuning` / detector
- `NEAR_ENTER_DBM` -55, `NEAR_EXIT_DBM` -63, new `LIST_ENTER_DBM` -78, `LIST_EXIT_DBM` -85, `CLOSE_HOLD_DBM` -52, `CLOSE_HOLD_MS` 350.
- `FireReason` gains `hold` (code `"hold"`).
- `DetectorState` gains `closeSinceMs` (initial -1; `CLOSE_USED` = -2). Per sample, after `near` is known and with `fastDbm = floorDiv(fastQ, 256)`:
  `close = !near ? -1 : (close == -2 ? -2 : (fastDbm >= CLOSE_HOLD_DBM ? (close == -1 ? t : close) : -1))`; a gap reset sets it to -1.
  After the jump paths: `if fire == none && near && cooled && close >= 0 && t - close >= CLOSE_HOLD_MS { fire = hold }`. On any fire: `close = -2` (together with the existing `held = -1`, `baselineQ = fastQ`).
  The golden step line is unchanged (`fire,glow,baselineQ,fastQ,near`).
- `SongTapNames` (new): `MAX_CHARS` 20, `ADVERT_NAME_MAX_BYTES` 24, `MAX_NAME_BYTES` 80, `FALLBACK` "Phone"; `clean`, `orFallback`, `isValid`, `advertName`, `fromAdvert`.
  `clean(raw)`: `SongTapText.sanitize(scalars, 80 bytes)`, cut to 20 scalars, sanitize again (drops a trailing space). `advertName(name)`: `sanitize(clean(name), 24 bytes)`. Scalars, not Swift `Character`s.
- `NearbyBook` (new): `update(id, name, role, fastQ, near, now)`, `remove`, `clear`, `ignore(id, now)`, `isIgnored`, `visible(role, now)`, `promptFor(role, now)`; constants `STALE_MS` 4000, `SAME_PHONE_GAP_MS` 1500, `IGNORE_MS` 60000.
  `listed` has hysteresis: stays listed while `dbm >= LIST_EXIT_DBM`, enters at `>= LIST_ENTER_DBM`; sort: dbm descending, then id ascending.
- `SongTapHandshake`: `GO_DELAY_MS` = 0; `senderStartMs` and `receiverStartMs` are gone.

`SongTapSession` (state machine)
- `SessionState` gains `target` (Int), `offerRead`, `tapSeen`, `acked`, `beamDone`, `pre` (Bool). `describe()` appends ` tgt=N`, ` read=1`, ` tapseen=1`, ` acked=1`, ` beamdone=1`, ` pre=1` only when set.
- `TimeoutTag` gains `target`. `TARGET_TIMEOUT_MS` = 60000.
- New events: `Approve(peer, videoId)`, `Republish(videoId)`, `OfferRead(peer, nowMs)`, `OfferReadLost(peer)`, `OfferPrefetched(peer, videoId, senderKey)`, `OfferGone(peer)`.
  `TapDetected` gets a third field `nowMs` (`tap(peer,vid,nowMs)`). `GoReceived` and the `SendGo` effect are gone.
- Sender: `Approve` (ARMED/FOUND/NEAR) -> OFFERED, `target = peer`, effects `publishOffer(peer,vid)`, `scheduleTimeout(target,60000)`. `OfferRead` sets `offerRead` (and commits if `tapSeen`).
  A tap for `target` commits when `offerRead`, else sets `tapSeen`; a tap with no target does nothing. Commit = TRANSFERRING with `haptic(tick)`, `startBeam(send,nowMs)`, `scheduleTimeout(ack,45000)`.
  `Ack` in OFFERED commits too (the receiver was faster); in TRANSFERRING it sets `acked` (and ends the session as SAVED if `beamDone`). `BeamFinished` ends the session if `acked` (or peer 0), else sets `beamDone`.
  `ACK` timeout is only for TRANSFERRING and not acked; `TARGET` timeout -> CANCELLED. `PeerLost` while armed (OFFERED with target) only clears `offerRead`; while TRANSFERRING and not acked it fails as before; after the ack it is nothing.
- Receiver: `OfferPrefetched` (FOUND/NEAR, same peer) stores `videoId`, `senderKey`, `pre`. `PeerLost` in FOUND/NEAR clears them. A tap with `pre` runs `admitOffer` (limits, then ask or save) with a `haptic(tick)` first;
  a tap without `pre` is the old read (`readOffer`, `scheduleTimeout(read)`, no tick) and the tick comes with the offer's arrival (peer != 0). Saving always emits `saveSong`, `scheduleTimeout(save,20000)`, `startBeam(receive,nowMs)`.
  A limit refusal over Bluetooth now also sends `sendDecline(peer)`.

### The wire (spec 5, additions to spec 4; `wire.jsonl`)

| | |
|---|---|
| send service | `b7f3a1c0-5d2e-4c9a-8e61-3f4d7a2b9c01` (unchanged) |
| receive service | `b7f3a1c0-5d2e-4c9a-8e61-3f4d7a2b9c02` (unchanged) |
| Info `...9c11`, Offer `...9c12`, Control `...9c13`, Event `...9c14` | unchanged (Hello 17 bytes, Ack 1, Decline 1, Tap 5) |
| **Name** `b7f3a1c0-5d2e-4c9a-8e61-3f4d7a2b9c15` | read, on the sender's server. Value = `0x01` then the clean name in UTF-8 (2 to 81 bytes, at most 20 scalars). Decode: version 1, strict UTF-8, run through `clean`, null when empty. |
| Event `0x02` (offer ready) | notification on Event: the sender has a new offer for this connected phone (sent to every central that has written Hello, when the listener says yes and again whenever the offer is made again). |
| Event `0x03` (offer withdrawn) | notification on Event: the sender has no offer any more (its listener cancelled, the 60 s ran out, the beam ended). A receiver holding an offer fetched ahead drops it (`OfferGone`). Sent to every central that has written Hello, when the sender withdraws an offer it had. |
| Event `0x01` (go) | reserved; never sent; ignored. |

**Advertisement and the short name.** The list shows names from advertisements only, so the short name travels without a connection:
- Android: advertisement data = the 128-bit role service UUID (as before); **scan response** = one manufacturer-specific block (AD type 0xFF), company id `0xFFFF` (bytes `FF FF`), data = `0x01` then
  `advertName(name)` in UTF-8 (at most 24 bytes; the block is at most 29 bytes of the 31). Never the system device name.
- iOS (a peripheral can advertise only its local name and service UUIDs, foreground): advertise the service UUID of the role and `CBAdvertisementDataLocalNameKey` = `advertName(name)`. iOS may shorten it further.
- Reading an advertisement, both platforms: if there is a manufacturer block with company id `0xFFFF` and version 1, use `decodeAdvertName(data)`; otherwise use the local name through `SongTapNames.fromAdvert`. Android reads iOS's name from `ScanRecord.getDeviceName()`; iOS reads Android's block from `CBAdvertisementDataManufacturerDataKey` (first two bytes are the company id, little endian).
- The full name (up to 20 scalars) comes from the Name characteristic once connected (receiver reads it right after connecting). A name is a label, never an identity: two phones can share one.
- A receiver has no GATT server, so a sender knows a receiver only by the short advertised name.

**The receiver's order after connecting** (all before the tap): write `Hello(challenge)`, subscribe to Event, read Name, read Offer. A read of Offer answered with ATT error `0x80` (no offer yet) is not an error: wait for Event `0x02`, and ask again every
`PREFETCH_RETRY_MS` = 2000 while connected and near and holding nothing. After every Offer read (good or bad) write a new `Hello`. Hold the checked offer; re-read it when it is older than `PREFETCH_MAX_AGE_MS` = 20000 or the sender notifies; drop it when
the connection closes, when Event `0x03` arrives, or when a read of Offer finds nothing (error `0x80`) while one is held. (Found by the first CI run: without `0x03` a receiver kept an offer
the sender had cancelled and a later hold would have saved it.) The sender makes the payload again every 20 s while armed (the payload carries a time) and on a song change; a receiver accepts a payload time up to 120 s old for a Bluetooth offer (the signed frame's own 30 s window still applies at read time).

**The sender's serving rule** (must be the same on iOS): serve `Offer` only to the central that wrote the Hello holding the challenge, only while the armed-to phone is the followed one, inside the strict near gate, with no other receiver inside it, and only to the first reader (until the offer is made again).
Accept `Tap`, `Ack`, `Decline` only from that reader (`Tap` also needs the sender's own signal jump of that phone within 1.5 s). A sender armed to a phone follows that phone only and does not switch to a stronger one; its tracking timeout for that phone is 10 s.

### Other constants (app side, `BleTuning`)

`PINNED_PEER_TIMEOUT_MS` 10000, `PREFETCH_RETRY_MS` 2000, `PREFETCH_MAX_AGE_MS` 20000, `NEARBY_PUBLISH_MS` 250, `ADVERT_TX_POWER` 1 (Android's LOW; iOS cannot set it), plus the spec 4 ones. An iPhone cannot lower its advertising power:
the strict gate is the only thing that keeps it from being connected to from far, and the phones read each other differently, so calibrate per pair with the diagnostics screen.

## Deviations and decisions

1. **No `go`** (see above). The brief allowed "a small clock-alignment mechanism that adds no visible delay"; none is needed because each phone starts at its own commit; skew is documented, not corrected.
2. **Advertising power is LOW, not ULTRA_LOW**, because the strict gate could not be calibrated here; ULTRA_LOW would put contact readings near the gate. One constant to change.
3. **Target by following, not by identity.** A GATT client's address is not its advertising address, so the sender cannot bind "this connection is the phone I picked". The substitute is: pin and follow the picked phone, serve only while it is inside the strict gate, and serve nobody when another receiver is inside it too. Two phones within the strict gate at once cannot both be served; the user moves one away.
4. **The sender must say yes first.** This makes the prefetch possible and doubles as the new consent. The old direct tap-to-send is gone for Bluetooth.
5. **Hold fire is a new third path, not a replacement**: the jump with jerk and the dwell (jump held 400 ms at -58 or stronger) stay; at a strong signal the hold (350 ms) simply comes first.
6. `OfferReadLost` and `PeerLost` both reach the sender when the reader drops; the core treats them the same while armed.
7. Calibration numbers are guesses (no logs existed).

## How to test with two phones

Two Androids (or the Pixel and an iPhone once the iOS port speaks spec 5), Bluetooth on, both signed in to Spotify, Song Tap allowed, a song with a Spotify id on the sender.

1. Settings > Song Tap > Your name: set two different names. Developer mode on: Settings > Song Tap > Song Tap diagnostics shows the gates.
2. Receiver: Song Tap > Receive. Sender: play a song, open Song Tap > Send (or: a song's options sheet > the round Song Drop button next to Share).
3. Sender: "Phones nearby" lists the receiver by name from a couple of metres ("Nearby"), not before. Check on the receiver's diagnostics: "gate now" = list only, "may connect and beam" = no, "connection" = none.
4. Bring the phones close. At the strict gate the sender asks "Song drop to X?" (and the row says "Close"); the receiver connects (diagnostics: connection, name, then "offer in hand" = yes after the sender says yes). Try No: that phone is not asked again for a minute.
5. Yes: "Tap phones together to share". Touch and hold gently (no bump): one haptic tick on each phone, the beam on both at once; the receiver's card says "From <sender>", "Adding", then "Added to your library" with Undo. First time it asks Add / Not now (the sender's beam already played; Not now shows "Not added" there).
6. Range: walk the phones apart while armed: nothing connects beyond the strict gate (diagnostics "gate now"); listing disappears after a few seconds beyond the list gate. Use the Mark buttons (touching, 1, 3, 10, 30 cm) and "Copy log" and send the numbers back: that is how `NEAR_ENTER_DBM`, `LIST_ENTER_DBM`, `CLOSE_HOLD_DBM` get set for real.
7. Both phones from the same iOS/Android mix: repeat in both directions; iOS-advertised names appear as the local name.
