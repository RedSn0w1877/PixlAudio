# PixlAudio Beta 3 (Android)

## New

- **Ask Taizo**: a music assistant you can chat with. Ask for songs, playlists or recommendations. When you're online it can also search the web and tune your taste for you.
- **Taste controls**: boost, play less of, mute or block any artist or song, on top of what the app learns from your listening. Blocked artists never show up in your mixes.
- **Listening history import**: bring in your history from Spotify (and some from YouTube Music) so recommendations fit you from day one.
- **Music haptics**: feel the beat through your phone's vibration.
- **New gestures**: double-tap the cover to like a song, long-press for quick actions, and swipe or long-press the mini player.
- **Find in playlist**: pull down or tap search to filter a playlist.
- **Accent color**: pick one for the whole app in Settings > Appearance.
- **Sing mode**: instrumental versions start within seconds with a one-minute preview, and "Prepare songs ahead" gets your next songs ready in advance.
- **Lyrics**: new Translate and Sing buttons, and the screen stays on while you read along.
- **Spotify Connect**: the volume buttons control your speaker, and the player shows where your music is playing.
- **Simpler Settings**: technical options are tucked behind Developer mode, and you choose whether processing happens on your phone or in the cloud.

## Changed

- A fresh Material 3 Expressive look, with a new logo and notification icon.
- Your music now comes from Spotify only. The app no longer scans songs stored on your phone.

## Fixed and faster

- Play, pause, skip and rewind work reliably again, even while a song is loading.
- A song whose video won't play is matched to another one instead of being skipped every time.
- Spotify import is faster and more reliable.
- Fixed a crash on startup.
- Songs start sooner, and the next song is prepared ahead of time (less on mobile data, none in Data Saver).
- Smoother scrolling and animations on Home, Search, the queue and Settings.
- Lyrics load faster and retry when they fail.
- A shareable diagnostics log in Settings > About helps you report problems.

## Good to know

- To import your Spotify history you may need to reconnect Spotify once.
- Beta 3 is signed with a new permanent key. If it won't install over your current version, uninstall the old one first. Future updates will install normally.
