# pixlaudio beta 3 (android)

ok so this ones pretty big ngl. heres whats in it

## new stuff

- ask taizo is here ye, its like a music assistant you can just chat with. ask it for songs or playlists or whatever and when youre online it can search the web and tune your taste for you
- taste controls: boost, play less of, mute or block any artist or song on top of what the app already learns from you. blocked artists never show up in your mixes
- you can import your listening history from spotify (and some youtube music) so the recs actually fit you from day one
- music haptics, you can like feel the beat through your phone vibrating
- new gestures: double tap the cover to like a song, long press for quick actions, and swipe or long press the mini player
- find in playlist, pull down or tap search to filter a playlist
- pick an accent color for the whole app in settings > appearance
- sing mode: instrumentals start in like seconds with a one minute preview, and prepare songs ahead gets your next songs ready early
- lyrics got translate and sing buttons and the screen stays on while youre reading along
- spotify connect: the volume buttons control your speaker now and the player shows where your music is playing
- settings are way simpler, the technical stuff is hidden behind developer mode and you pick if stuff gets processed on your phone or in the cloud

## changed

- fresh material 3 expressive look with a new logo and notification icon
- your music comes from spotify only now, the app doesnt scan songs on your phone anymore

## fixed + faster

- play, pause, skip and rewind actually work again, even while a song is loading
- if a songs video wont play it gets matched to another one instead of getting skipped every time
- spotify import is faster and breaks way less
- fixed a crash on startup
- songs start sooner and the next one gets ready ahead of time (less on mobile data, none in data saver)
- scrolling and animations are smoother on home, search, the queue and settings
- lyrics load faster and retry when they fail
- theres a diagnostics log you can share in settings > about if something breaks

## good to know

- you might need to reconnect spotify once to import your history
- beta 3 uses a new permanent signing key so if it wont install over your current version just uninstall the old one first. updates after this one install normally
