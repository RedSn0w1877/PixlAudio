# Android: Material 3 Expressive instead of Liquid Glass

Branch `android-m3e`. Three commits on top of `c74a9a9`.

## What this is

The Liquid Glass visual style was ported to Android alongside the iOS app. It is now gone from
Android. The iOS-derived **layouts** stay; only the glass **material** is replaced, by stock
Material 3 / Material 3 Expressive components over the tonal `surfaceContainer*` roles.

iOS is untouched and keeps its own Liquid Glass.

## What replaced it

`AdaptiveM3.kt` takes over from `GlassAdaptive.kt`. The `Adaptive*` wrappers
(`AdaptiveModalBottomSheet`, `AdaptiveAlertDialog`, `AdaptiveDialogSurface`,
`AdaptiveFullScreenDialog`, `AdaptiveClickableSurface`, `AdaptiveClickableCard`,
`AdaptivePressSurface`, `AdaptivePressCard`) used to pick a glass container or a Material one;
they now call the Material component straight through. They are kept rather than inlined because
call sites pass a nullable `sheetState` / `contentWindowInsets`, which Material's own signatures
do not accept.

Every `if (glassMode) { … } else { … }` pair across the UI collapsed to its Material branch. The
queue sheet was the largest of these: sheet container, header, source badge, toolbar, the ⋯ menu
and the undo bar all now use the Material path only.

`AdaptiveChip` and `RestoreMaterialColors` had no Material equivalent to fall back to — their
Material rendering already sat inline at the call site — so the three call sites in `StatsScreen`
and `SettingsCategoryScreen` were unwrapped instead of keeping pass-through shims.

## Layouts that stay

Player top bar, the lyrics page with **Translate** and **Sing**, the Connect sheet, the Active
jobs entry, the accent picker (Settings › Appearance › Accent Color) and Cloud processing. These
are the iOS-derived layouts; they kept their structure and lost only their glass surfaces.

## Performance work kept

All of it. The player-scheme restart scopes (`PlayerSchemeScopes.kt`), the draw-time colour read
in `Modifier.drawBackgroundOutline`, the marquee and album-colour-fade fixes, the album-tile
first-compose colours, the folder-path and cover-extraction caching, and the
`PersistentList<Song>` queue handling are untouched.

## Build

`./gradlew :app:compileDebugKotlin` — **green**.
`./gradlew :app:testDebugUnitTest` — **green**.

Two environment notes for whoever builds next in this worktree:

- The worktree had no `local.properties`; one was created pointing at
  `C:\Users\Hoa\AppData\Local\Android\Sdk` with the same `SPOTIFY_CLIENT_ID` as the main checkout.
  It is gitignored.
- Gradle could not delete its own `app/build` intermediates under the agent sandbox, which
  surfaces as `java.io.IOException: Unable to delete directory … packageDebugResources`. It is not
  a code failure. Build outside the sandbox, or move `app/build` aside once.

## Deliberately left out

- **Six `run { … }` wrappers** remain where an `if (glassMode)` branch was removed and the
  Material branch kept its block (`AccentColorSettingItem`, `MainActivity`, and four others).
  They are no-ops that compile and behave identically; unwrapping them means re-indenting whole
  composable bodies, which is churn with real risk for no behaviour change. Worth a follow-up
  formatting pass, not worth doing alongside a 13 k-line removal.
- **No on-device verification.** Everything below is compile-verified only.

## What to test on-device

1. **Queue sheet** — the biggest rewrite. Open it, scroll, drag to dismiss, reorder with the
   handle, swipe a row away and hit **Undo** on the snackbar, then the ⋯ menu's three actions
   (Locate, Clear queue, Save as playlist). Check the header, the source badge and the toolbar
   circles all have real Material surfaces and no transparent gaps.
2. **Full player** — the top bar, transport controls and the output pill; skip tracks and watch
   the album-colour fade still animate smoothly (the restart scopes are perf-critical here).
3. **Lyrics page** — Translate and Sing, the floating toolbar, the alignment picker, the More
   sheet.
4. **Accent picker** — Settings › Appearance › Accent Color: the swatch grid, a preset, the custom
   hex sheet's Cancel/Apply (Apply must be disabled on a bad hex), and that the choice re-themes
   the app, the widgets and the watch.
5. **Settings** — every category screen, the dialogs, and confirm the **Visual style** row is gone
   from Experimental settings with nothing broken where it sat.
6. **Stats** — the two `FilterChip` rows (metric and category) select correctly; their wrapper was
   removed, so this is the likeliest place for a layout regression.
7. **Connect sheet, Cloud processing, Active jobs** — all still reachable and rendering.
8. **Light and dark**, and a non-dynamic accent, since glass used to supply its own palette.
